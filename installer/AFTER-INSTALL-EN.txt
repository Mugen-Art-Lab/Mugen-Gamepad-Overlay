Mugen Gamepad Overlay 0.8.0

1. Start OBS Studio.
2. Add a new source.
3. Choose Mugen Gamepad Overlay.
4. The built-in universal skin works without additional files.
5. Input Overlay and GamepadViewer skins are downloaded separately.

Documentation:
https://github.com/Mugen-Art-Lab/Mugen-Gamepad-Overlay
